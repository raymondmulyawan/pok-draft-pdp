Founded in Burgundy – the food and wine heartland of France, in 1850 - the family-run cookware brand of Emile Henry, is always designing new pieces that are useful, beautiful and durable.

Discover the Sublime collection, which is crafted with new, versatile ceramic technology. It withstands the high heat of searing, frying or barbecuing, but also allows for gentle cooking at low temperatures, so the sugars in a caramel sauce or custard are gently heated without burning or overcooking. It renders every meal – sublime.

Features:

  • Handcrafted in France
  • Crafted in 100% natural ceramic
  • Ceramic lead cadmium and nickel free
  • Distributes heat smoothly throughout the pan
  • 50% lighter than most other Dutch ovens
  • Suitable for gas, ceramic, halogen & electric hobs
  • Maximum temperature 290 °C with a minimum of -20°C
  • Microwave and dishwasher safe
  • 10-year guarantee
  • Presented in Emile Henry packaging
Design: Graphite
